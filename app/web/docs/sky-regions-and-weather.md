# Sky, regions and weather

*Which region the outdoors belongs to, the weather and sounds it brings, previewing the sky and time of day,
and an interior's light.*

## Regions

Every outdoor cell can belong to a **region**: the Ashlands, the Bitter Coast, the West Gash… A region decides
the **weather** the cell gets, its **background sounds**, what may **attack you when you sleep** there, and the
name shown as you walk in. The land shaping tools also learn each region's character from its cells.

**On the World map** (outdoors, **Sky** panel):

- **This cell is in:** pick its region (or none). **✎** opens the region's settings.
- **Show regions on the map** colours every cell by its region.
- **🖌 Paint regions…**: pick a region in the bar at the top, then click or drag over cells to put them in it.
  **(no region: erase)** takes cells out. **Done** stops painting.
- **🌦 All regions** opens the list on the Rules page.

## A region's settings

**Rules → 🌦 Regions & weather** lists every region, with how many cells it has. Click one, or **＋ New** for a new one:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Name | Shown when the player walks into the region. |
| Colour on the map | Its colour on the World map (and the game's own map). |
| Sleep encounters | A leveled creature list: what may attack when the player sleeps outdoors here. Empty for nothing. |
| Weather | The chance of each weather: Clear, Cloudy, Foggy, Overcast, Rain, Thunderstorm, Ash storm, Blight, and (with Bloodmoon) Snow and Blizzard. They must add up to 100%; **Make it 100%** fixes it. |
| Background sounds | Each plays now and then outdoors in the region; the number is its chance. Up to 32. **▶** listens. |
| 🖌 Paint cells on the World map | Opens the map painting this region. |

## Previewing the sky

In 3D outdoors, the game's own sky is drawn: clouds, sun, moons and stars (**👁 View ▾ → Sky** turns it off).

- **Weather preview** (Sky panel) shows how the sky, fog and falling rain, ash or snow look for each weather.
  In the game, the weather is picked from the region's chances.
- **Time of day**: drag through the day to see sunrise, day, sunset and night in the game's own colours for that
  weather. Lamps and torches count for more as it gets dark. **☀ Plain daylight** goes back to even light to
  work by.
- **💡 Game light / ☀ Bright** in the 3D toolbar switches between the game's lighting and bright, even light.

## An interior's light

Indoors, the **Sky** panel has the place's **Lighting & settings**: a mood to start from, ambient, sunlight and fog
colours, fog thickness, water, **Behaves like outdoors** (weather and sky inside, as in Mournhold) and **Players
can't sleep here**. See *Furnishing and checking* for each.

## Good to know

- A region painted on cells is part of your mod, like any other change; one Undo takes back a painted stroke.
- Land shaping's **Dressing → Put the cells in this region** paints the shaped cells into a region as part of the
  same change.
- Sounds for regions come from the **Sounds** list on the Rules page; make a new one from any sound file in your
  game or your Library.
