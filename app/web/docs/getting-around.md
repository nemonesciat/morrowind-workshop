# Getting around

*Your mod, the pages along the top, finding any record, and working faster.*

## Your mod

The Workshop reads OpenMW's own settings, so it sees your Morrowind files and the mods you have switched on,
in the same order as the game. **It never changes them.** Everything you make or change goes into a mod of
your own.

- **＋ Start a mod** (on Home) gives it a name. Its name then shows at the top, with how many changes it has:
  click it for **My mod**. **Open another…** on Home switches to another mod or starts a new one.
- There is **no Save button**: every change is saved as you make it.
- **Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last change, **Redo** (Ctrl+Y) puts it back. Most tools make one change at a
  time, so one Undo takes back a whole furnished room or a shaped valley.
- The Workshop also keeps **backups** of your mod while you work (Settings → Backups) and can restore any of
  them. It backs up the mod as it is first, so you can come back.

## The pages

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Home (the logo) | Where to begin, a guided first house, the places you were lately. |
| Browse | Every record in your game: search, read, change. |
| World map | Places in 2D or 3D: build, place, furnish, shape the land, Play here. |
| Create | Quick starts: a house, a dungeon, people, items, spells, books, loot lists, and the Forge. |
| Library | Your own models and textures. |
| Characters | People and creatures: looks, stats, what they sell, where they stand. |
| Dialogue | Greetings, topics and quests with their journal. |
| Scripts | Morrowind scripts and OpenMW Lua scripts, checked as you type. |
| Rules | Factions, classes, races, birthsigns, skills, regions, sounds, magic effects, globals, game settings. |
| My mod | What your mod contains; checking and packing it to share. |
| Health check | Problems in your load order, and a check of every record of your mod. |
| Load order | Switch plugins on or off and change their order. |

The buttons at the top right: **? Help** (this guide and the others), **▶ OpenMW** (start the game, its Launcher,
the load order), **Undo/Redo**, **↻ Reload** (re-read your openmw.cfg and all plugins), and while the game runs
from the Workshop, **Me** (show where your character is).

## Browse: finding any record

Type in the search box at the top: names, IDs, dialogue lines, model paths. Narrow it with the categories on
the left. Click a result to read it.

- **▦ Table** shows the list as columns you choose (**Columns ▾**) and sort (click a heading).
- A record shows **which files define it**, in load order, and the one the game uses. Click an older one to
  compare.
- Underlined values are links: click to open that record. **← →** go back and forward.
- **Where it's used** lists the places it stands in, who carries it, and the scripts and dialogue that name it.
  **F1** on anything shows the same.
- **✎ Edit** changes it in your mod. Only the fields you change are saved; the original files stay untouched.
  **⧉ Use as template** makes a copy under a new ID.
- **📍 Place on the map** (on anything that can stand in the world) starts placing it on the World map; **🔨 Add
  to the workbench** puts it on the prefab workbench.
- Records with an editor of their own (people, items, spells, lists, scripts, factions, classes…) have a button
  that opens it.
- **Undo my changes** (or **Delete from my mod**) takes a record back to how the game has it.

## Panels and layouts

Every page's tools are **panels**.

- **Drag a tab** to move it: dock it on a side, tab it with others, or float it.
- **Drag the edges** to resize. **⋮** floats or closes a panel.
- **▤ Panels** opens any panel and has ready-made layouts. You can save your own.
- Groups of buttons over the map have a grip **⋮⋮**: drag it to put the group where you like. Right-click a grip
  to put them back in a row, or lock them.

## Working faster

- **Ctrl+K** finds any command, page, panel, place or record.
- **Ctrl+/** changes the keyboard shortcuts.
- **Right-click** anything on the World map, in 3D or in a list for what you can do with it.
- **F1** on something shows where it's used.
- The bar under the map shows the place, the mouse or camera position (click to copy it), and what's selected.

## Settings

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Appearance | Themes, and colours of your own. |
| OpenMW | Where OpenMW is installed, and its settings folder. **Choose OpenMW's folder…** if it wasn't found. |
| AI assistant | Optional. Your own Anthropic API key, stored only on this PC; each request is billed to your Anthropic account. Everything else works without it. |
| Backups of your mod | How often, how many to keep, and **Restore**. |
| Starting up | Open on Home or on Browse. |
| Folders | Your projects and the Workshop's own folder. |

## The Workshop learning

The first time, the Workshop studies the interiors of your installed game (a few minutes, on your PC, no AI,
no internet). That's where its building and furnishing know-how comes from. The **📚 Learning** button at the top
shows how far it is; tools that need it wait and say so. **? Help → How the Workshop learns** explains it all.

## If something goes wrong

- **Undo** takes back changes one by one; **Settings → Backups** restores an older copy of your mod.
- **? Help → Report a problem** makes a report with the details needed to fix it, ready to copy and send.
- **? Help → Recent messages** shows the messages that appeared at the bottom, to read again.
